# Bangladesh Research — Academy / Self-Development / Course / Book Sites

**Purpose:** what Bangladeshi academy, self-development, online-course and Islamic-learning
sites actually do on their homepages, and which of their sections/features are worth
borrowing for Energy Mining Academy — and which to avoid.
**Researched:** 2026-11-25. Method: `browser.search` to find live sites, then `browser.open`
to read each homepage (and, where useful, a course/book listing page) directly.
Everything below is what was actually read on the page or in the search result text;
nothing is invented. Where a page could not be read fully, that is stated.

**Our site, for reference:** Energy Mining Academy (Sayful Alvi) — self-knowledge →
self-change. Centre: Human Assessment (210 questions, 7 layers × 30) + 26 self-tests,
Four Energy courses (Voice / Body / Mind / Purpose), books, Personal Project Plan,
spiritual lessons. Audience: Bangladesh, all levels, Muslim + non-Muslim, adults / teens /
children, mostly mobile, slow internet. Courses + books SELL and lead the homepage;
assessment is **COMPLETELY FREE** (no paywall cues, no price) — its own menu section +
exactly ONE homepage section. Books = Rokomari-style shop. Courses = multi-teacher
platform. Brand colours only: Navy #032C42, Off-white #F6F3EC, Gold #C9A24B,
Charcoal #1C1C1E. Bilingual bn/en, Bangla first.

**Sites studied (7):** 10 Minute School, Shikho, Ostad, Interactive Cares,
Ghoori Learning, Rokomari, Islamic Online Madrasah (IOM).
**Attempted but not fully readable:** Bohubrihi (bohubrihi.com) — the homepage text fetch
returned only the footer (support centre, helpline 16780, social links); the site is
JavaScript-heavy. Secondary sources (company profiles, a TBS feature) describe it as a
Bangla professional-course platform (web development, digital marketing, Python, data
analytics; one-time payment, lifetime access; some free courses) acquired by Shikho in
November 2021. Because the homepage itself could not be read, it is not given a full
section below.

## 1. 10 Minute School — 10minuteschool.com

**What it is:** Bangladesh's largest online classroom — school/college academic
preparation (Class 5–12, SSC, HSC, admission), plus English and skill courses.
Bangla-first homepage.

**Homepage sections actually seen (read 2026-11-25):**
- Hero: "শেখা হোক আনন্দে!" (Let learning be joyful) — "এক জায়গায় স্কুল ও কলেজের
  সম্পূর্ণ প্রস্তুতি!" — followed immediately by a row of audience cards (HSC ২৬,
  HSC ২৭, HSC ২৮, ১০ম শ্রেণি, ৯ম শ্রেণি, ৫ম থেকে ৮ম শ্রেণি). The visitor picks
  *their own level* first.
- English-learning section (online + offline centres) with an "এক্সপ্লোর করুন" button.
- "অনলাইনেই শেখা হোক নিজের গতিতে!" — three product cards: Super Live Class (live
  classes with university students/alumni + national ranking), SuperPrep (daily/weekly
  quizzes, MCQ bank, full model tests, find your weaknesses), TenTen (24/7 learning
  partner / doubt-solving AI).
- Offline section: "অফলাইনে শেখা, আরও ইন্টারঅ্যাকটিভভাবে!" — teacher panel,
  1-1 counselling for parents, learn-by-doing activities/games.
- "দেশসেরা সকল অনলাইন ব্যাচ" — batch cards (Class 6–8, SSC, HSC online batches),
  with subject chips for HSC 2028 (Physics, Chemistry, Math, Biology, Bangla,
  English, ICT, Finance, Accounting, Management, Marketing, Geography, Economics,
  Civics).
- "অনলাইন ব্যাচের শিক্ষার্থীরা পাচ্ছে" — benefit list: 10 weekly live classes,
  printed masterbook + extra notes, unlimited MCQ practice, recorded classes if you
  miss, homework + weekly exams, 24-hour doubt solving.
- Photo gallery ("image আর শেখার সেই পথেই গড়ে উঠছে আত্মবিশ্বাসী সফল প্রজন্ম").
- "কেন আমরাই শিক্ষার্থী ও অভিভাবকদের প্রথম পছন্দ?" — student/parent testimonial
  cards with name, class/school, and a quoted story.
- App download section: Google Play / App Store buttons, "৪.৫+ রেটিং দিয়েছে প্রায়
  ৩,০০,০০০ শিক্ষার্থী", offline-download feature ("পড়া চলবে ইন্টারনেট ছাড়াও"),
  24×7 doubt-solving, helpline 16910 (24×7).
- A separate IELTS landing page (seen via search) shows the course-detail pattern:
  course title, lecture count, mock-test count, free hardcopy book, instructor bio
  with credentials, FAQ, "Enroll Now".

**Worth borrowing — and why:**
- **"Pick your level" hero cards.** The homepage opens by letting the visitor
  self-select (HSC 26 / Class 10 / …). For us: a "Where are you starting from?"
  row — e.g. "নিজেকে জানতে চাই / কণ্ঠস্বর / শরীর / মন / উদ্দেশ্য" — so a visitor
  lands on their own journey instead of reading a generic pitch. Fits our 6-stage
  journey and the four energies.
- **"What you get" benefit list per programme.** Concrete, checkable items
  (live classes, notes, practice, recordings). Our course pages should do the same:
  what each Four Energy course contains (lessons, live sessions, practice, notes),
  not vague promises.
- **Free companion product as a trust bridge.** TenTen (24/7 doubt partner) and
  free suggestion/model-test content sit beside paid batches. Analogue for us: the
  free self-tests / free Human Assessment as the trust bridge that sits next to
  paid courses and books — free first, selling second, exactly as our plan says.
- **Offline access note.** "Download and watch without internet" matters for slow
  internet. Our audio lessons / book PDFs should promise the same where true.

**Avoid — and why:**
- **National ranking / leaderboard language** ("জাতীয় র‍্যাঙ্কিংয়ে নিজের অবস্থান
  দেখুন"). Our plan forbids ranking and single total scores on assessments; a
  competitive rank would contradict the self-knowledge (not comparison) purpose.
- **Testimonial wall as social proof.** Long named student stories with schools and
  ranks. Our plan forbids fake testimonials; we have no verified learner stories
  yet, so no testimonial section until real ones exist.
- **Exam-batch machinery** (weekly live classes, MCQ banks, model tests, printed
  masterbooks). Built for SSC/HSC exam prep; our audience is self-development,
  not exam cramming. Do not copy the batch/exam structure.
- **Heavy hero imagery / many banners.** The homepage is image-dense; on slow
  mobile internet that is a liability for us. Keep our pages light.

## 2. Shikho — shikho.com

**What it is:** Online academic platform (Class 6–10, SSC, HSC, university admission),
Bangla-first, app-led. Homepage headline: "৩০ লক্ষ+ শিক্ষার্থীর আস্থা" (trust of
30 lakh+ students).

**Homepage sections actually seen (read 2026-11-25):**
- Hero: "একাডেমিক থেকে এডমিশন — প্রস্তুতি নাও দেশ সেরা শিক্ষক ও প্রযুক্তির সাথে",
  with trust numbers (30 lakh+ students, 20+ experienced mentors, 45 lakh+ app
  downloads, 5 lakh+ learning materials).
- "Shikho একাডেমিক প্রোগ্রামে যা যা থাকছে" — feature grid with icons: live and
  recorded classes, animated videos, practice MCQ tests, live MCQ tests, class
  notes, smart notes, report card.
- Animated video-lesson section: "দারুণ সব অ্যানিমেটেড উদাহরণের ভিডিও লেসনে শেখা
  হবে আরও সহজ" — sample lessons listed by class/subject (e.g. SSC Physics 8.1,
  SSC Chemistry 1.1, HSC Biology 2.8, SSC Math 16.1) with class/subject tabs.
- "কেন Shikho-তে আস্থা রাখবে?" — four trust points: best content, easy study
  material, low cost for a lot, fluent presentation.
- Testimonials: named students with results (e.g. medical admission rank,
  SSC 1st place with marks) and quotes.
- Investors and partners logos section.
- Footer/contact: helpline 16780, team@shikho.com, Dhaka office address.

**Worth borrowing — and why:**
- **"What the programme includes" icon grid.** Seven plain items (live +
  recorded class, animated video, practice test, live test, notes, smart notes,
  report card) — a visitor understands the product in one glance. For us: the
  same treatment for a Four Energy course ("what you get") and for the free
  Human Assessment (what the report contains — four energies, strengths, type,
  where care is needed — without a score).
- **Sample-lesson preview by category.** Short real lessons, filterable by
  class/subject, so visitors taste the teaching before enrolling. For us: a free
  sample lesson / sample self-test / sample report preview for each course and
  for the assessment — try before you buy, and try the free assessment before
  anything.
- **Trust numbers kept small and factual.** Four numbers, no hype adjectives.
  We should only ever show numbers we can verify (e.g. 210 questions, 7 layers,
  26 self-tests, 4 courses, 1 book so far).

**Avoid — and why:**
- **Rank/result testimonials** (admission ranks, "SSC 1st in the country with
  1285 marks"). Directly against our no-ranking / no-score rule, and we have no
  such stories.
- **"Report card" framing.** A report card implies grading. Our assessment report
  is a map, not a grade — never present it as a report card.
- **Academic-only structure.** Everything is organised by school class and exam;
  our journey is by life stage and energy, not by exam year.

## 3. Ostad — ostad.app

**What it is:** Live skill-development platform (30+ live courses: AI, web/app
development, product/design, business/marketing, data, cyber security). Bangla-first
homepage. Tagline: "বাংলাদেশ শিখবে লাইভে" (Bangladesh will learn live).

**Homepage sections actually seen (read 2026-11-25):**
- Hero: "স্কিল শেখার মাধ্যমে বদলে ফেলুন নিজের ভবিষ্যৎ" + graduate-employment
  strip ("১০০০০+ গ্র্যাজুয়েটরা কর্মরত আছেন বিভিন্ন দেশি ও বিদেশি প্রতিষ্ঠানে").
- "আপকামিং লাইভ কোর্স" — course categories with course counts: Artificial
  Intelligence (3 courses), Web & App Development (5), Product Management &
  Design (1), Business & Marketing (1), Data Engineering (3), Networking &
  Cyber Security (2).
- "ফ্রি লাইভ ডেমো ক্লাস" — a list of dated free demo classes (topic + day/date +
  time, e.g. Tuesday 24 November, 9:00 PM), each a real upcoming session.
- "Ostad for business" — corporate training section.
- Free tools as lead magnets: "সিভি বানিয়ে ফেলুন একদম ফ্রি তে" (free CV builder)
  and "কোডিং নলেজ এক্সপ্লোর করুন CodeMama তে" (free coding-knowledge tool).
- Recorded demo classes + crash courses section.
- "কি কি পাচ্ছেন লাইভ কোর্সে" — benefit grid: dedicated job-placement team,
  CV builder + expert CV review, 18 hours live support (specific courses),
  special CV/job support in Pro batch, live tests to check yourself, up to 3
  support classes a day (specific courses).
- Numbers strip: job placement, learners, course-completion rate, live courses.
- Graduate testimonials: name, course + batch, quoted experience (several in
  Bangla, several in English).
- App download section.
- A course-detail page (seen via search, "AI Driven Data Analytics Career Track"):
  free live demo class signup (mobile + email for link), curriculum by module
  with teacher names, instructor bio with credentials, tools taught, "what you
  need" (laptop/RAM/internet), "what you get" (study plan, live classes, real-life
  projects, progress tracking, support classes, community, lifetime access, job
  guide, interview handbook, certificate), FAQ, how-to-pay.

**Worth borrowing — and why:**
- **Category cards with counts.** Six categories, each showing how many courses
  it holds. For us: the four energies as four cards (Voice / Body / Mind /
  Purpose) with course counts — the cleanest possible map of our catalogue.
- **Free live demo class before enrolment.** A dated, real free session as the
  try-before-you-buy step. For us: a free introductory session / free sample
  lesson per course, and the free self-tests as the equivalent "try it free"
  step before any paid course.
- **Free tools as lead magnets.** A free CV builder and a free knowledge tool
  sit on the homepage and bring people in without selling. Direct analogue: our
  free self-tests and free Human Assessment are our lead magnets — they deserve
  the same prominent, generous treatment (one homepage section, own menu entry),
  never hidden behind a paywall.
- **Course-detail page anatomy.** Demo class → curriculum by module with teacher
  → instructor bio → tools/requirements → "what you get" → FAQ → payment. This
  is the template for our course detail pages (with our own content: lessons,
  practice, teacher, what you get; no payment UI for now per owner order).
- **"What you get in the live course" benefit grid.** Same lesson as 10 Minute
  School/Shikho, but tied to live learning (support, tests, projects).

**Avoid — and why:**
- **Job-placement / employment promises** ("10000+ graduates employed…",
  dedicated placement team, CV/job support). Our courses promise self-change,
  not jobs; copying employment claims would be false for us.
- **Urgency mechanics on the course list** (seen on the listing page via search:
  "seats left", "days left" per course). Countdown/scarcity pressure does not fit
  a calm self-knowledge brand and our plan forbids timers.
- **Completion-rate / learner-count numbers** unless verified. We must not show
  placement or completion percentages we do not have.

## 4. Interactive Cares — interactivecares.com

**What it is:** Upskilling + job-placement platform (career paths and live/recorded
courses: AI, MERN/web, DevOps, data, digital marketing, languages, BCS).
Tagline: "Upskill for a Better Career".

**Homepage sections actually seen (read 2026-11-25; the fetch returned the lower
half of the page — hero and course grid were not in the returned text, so they are
described from the search-result course listings instead):**
- "How it works" in 4 steps: 1) Create an account, 2) Select course/career path,
  3) Enrol in the course, 4) Open course dashboard.
- "কী বলছেন আমাদের সাকসেসফুল লার্নাররা" — successful-learner stories.
- "Our Students Success Story" section.
- "কেন Interactive Cares বেস্ট চয়েস?" — positioning copy ("not just a skill
  platform; a complete gateway from career journey start to job-market placement"),
  with a benefit list: affordable price, best mentor panel, industry-focused
  outlines, industry-standard projects/quizzes/assignments, exclusive support
  session per course, special project day, certificate on completion, job-
  preparation support, job placement opportunity in 110+ partner companies.
- Newsletter section: "Boost Your Learning Journey!" — updates on new courses,
  workshops, resources; expert tips; job openings; discounts/early access.
- "Interactive Cares in Media" — awards & recognition.
- "We are partnering industry top leaders" (partners).
- "Hassle Free Hiring" — B2B hiring section (5,00,000+ pre-screened candidates,
  10,000+ placements, 130+ hiring-partner companies).
- "Get Your Free Consultation Today!" — free consultation CTA (1500+ students got
  consultation).
- App download, contact (Mohammadpur head office + Dhanmondi learning centre),
  payment methods.
- Course listings (seen via search results): each course card shows mode
  (Live / Recorded / Online / Offline), batch number, student count, star rating
  with review count, and price with a struck-through original price and a % off.
  Course categories include Career Paths, Learning Paths, IELTS, plus a "Free
  Course" category; an older version of the site (interactivecares-courses.com)
  shows a category picker ("Select Your Favourite Category And Start Learning"),
  a "Most Trending Online Courses" row, and "Add to cart" on course cards.

**Worth borrowing — and why:**
- **4-step "how it works".** Create account → choose path → enrol → open
  dashboard. For us: a 4-step strip for the journey — "নিজেকে জানুন → পরিকল্পনা
  করুন → শিখুন → বদলান" — or for the free assessment flow (start → answer →
  see your map → choose a next step). Simple, mobile-friendly, no jargon.
- **"Career Path" vs single course.** Bundled paths (a sequence of courses toward
  one goal) rather than only standalone courses. For us: the 6-stage journey IS
  the path — present the Four Energy courses as a path (e.g. assessment → one
  energy → next energy → Personal Project Plan), not as four disconnected
  products.
- **Category picker as the catalogue front door.** "Select your favourite category
  and start learning" with a visible category list. For us: energies + books +
  free tests as the catalogue front door.
- **Free consultation CTA.** A free, human first step before any purchase. For
  us: the free assessment and free self-tests play this role — a no-cost first
  conversation with yourself before buying a course or book.

**Avoid — and why:**
- **Discount-led pricing display.** Every course card shows a struck-through price
  and a % off (20–80% off in the listings seen). Constant discounting cheapens a
  premium self-development brand; our plan says bundles not discounts in spirit,
  and our prices are not even set yet — never show invented discounts.
- **Job-placement machinery** (110+ partner companies, hiring section, "Hassle
  Free Hiring" B2B). Not our business; our outcome is self-change.
- **Star ratings + review counts on course cards** (e.g. 4.8 (80)). We have no
  verified reviews; showing ratings would be invented social proof.
- **Newsletter promising "discounts / early access / perks".** A newsletter is
  fine; a discount-driven one is not our model.

## 5. Ghoori Learning — ghoorilearning.com

**What it is:** Skill-development platform for Bangla speakers (180+ video courses:
IT, business, design, photography, digital marketing, SEO, freelancing, English/
IELTS, and more), started from BBC Janala English. Founded 2019; app-first.

**Homepage sections actually seen (read 2026-11-25; the fetch returned only part
of the page — the course grid was not in the returned text):**
- "বুঝতে পারছেন না কোথা থেকে শুরু করবেন? চিন্তার কিছু নেই! আমাদের কুইজ
  সেগমেন্ট আপনাকে পৌঁছে দেবে সঠিক সিদ্ধান্তে।" — a **quiz that tells you where
  to start**, with a "কুইজ শুরু করুন" button.
- "টিচার হতে চান?" — "Become an instructor and change lives — including your
  own." with a "Get started" button.
- "Want to earn 5 Ghoori Learning points? Answer these 5 questions to assess
  yourself and get a bonus 5 points." — a **5-question self-assessment quiz**
  with a points reward ("Take the quiz now").
- Footer: Dotlines Bangladesh Ltd, Gulshan address, phone 09-642601771.
- From the app description (Google Play, seen via search): 180+ video courses in
  Bangla from industry experts, certificates on completion; **a free BBC Janala
  English test** — "take our free BBC Janala English test, assess your English
  proficiency and choose your courses according to your need"; sign-up with
  Facebook/Google or name + mobile + password; dashboard shows enrolled courses
  and progress.

**Worth borrowing — and why:**
- **"Don't know where to start? Take the quiz."** This is the single most
  relevant idea in all the research. A short quiz that routes a confused visitor
  to the right starting point is exactly what our free Human Assessment and
  self-tests do — only ours is deeper and genuinely free. Our homepage should
  have this routing question ("কোথা থেকে শুরু করবেন?") pointing at the free
  assessment/self-tests, and the assessment result should route onward to the
  right energy course or book.
- **Free diagnostic test as the front door.** The free BBC Janala English test
  ("assess your proficiency, then choose your courses") is the model for our
  free assessment: free first, and the result — not a salesperson — recommends
  the next step. No paywall cue anywhere near it.
- **"Become an instructor" section.** Multi-teacher platform needs a teacher
  entry point. For us: a "শিক্ষক হতে চান?" section/page — teachers set their own
  pricing and enrolment (per our plan) and get their own dashboard.
- **Progress on the dashboard.** Enrolled courses + progress status visible after
  sign-in. For us: "My courses / My results / My orders" (already in our page
  list) with journey-stage progress.

**Avoid — and why:**
- **Points/gamification for answering questions** ("earn 5 points for 5
  questions"). Rewarding self-reflection with points turns self-knowledge into a
  game and invites gaming the answers; our assessments must stay sincere and
  un-gamified, with no score.
- **App-first, web-thin.** The website homepage returned very little content;
  the real product lives in the app (which was removed from Google Play in May
  2026, per a store listing seen in search). Our site must work fully on the
  mobile web — no app dependency.
- **180+ course catalogue sprawl.** A huge undifferentiated catalogue. We have
  4 courses and 1 book; our strength is focus, not volume — never pad the
  catalogue.

## 6. Rokomari — rokomari.com

**What it is:** Bangladesh's largest online bookstore (300,000+ books in ~3,000
categories, per its own homepage text seen via search), plus electronics/gadgets/
stationery. The model for our bookshop section. Bangla-first.

**Sections actually seen:**
- Book listing page (`/book`, read 2026-11-25): an enormous browse structure —
  **by category** (e.g. বেস্ট সেলার বুকস; আত্ম-উন্নয়ন, মোটিভেশনাল ও মেডিটেশন;
  ধর্মীয় বই; শিশু-কিশোর বই; ইতিহাস ও ঐতিহ্য; উপন্যাস; জীবনী; ব্যবসা, বিনিয়োগ
  ও অর্থনীতি; পরিবার ও প্যারেন্টিং; প্রি অর্ডার; অফার; ইন স্টক বুকস), **by author**
  (long alphabetical author list, Bangla and international), and **by publisher**.
  Categories are the primary navigation; author and publisher are secondary
  browse paths.
- Homepage text (seen via search): "Rokomari.com : The Largest Online Store in
  Bangladesh" — 300,000+ books, cash on delivery, "Happy return policy", free
  shipping offer; separate sections for book categories and for other product
  categories.
- Seller/pre-order page (seen via search): pre-order products organised by the
  same category tree, plus "By Authors", "By Publishers", "By Languages"
  (বাংলা / ইংরেজি / আরবি / উর্দু…), and price filtering.
- Customer behaviour (seen in Facebook-post search results, not the site itself):
  buyers ask about specific titles, stock, delivery fees (tiered by Dhaka /
  outside Dhaka / union), and order by giving name, phone and full address;
  the shop replies with links, prices and ordering steps. Cash on delivery is
  the expected payment mode.

**Worth borrowing — and why:**
- **Browse by category, by author, by publisher.** Three browse paths, with
  category first. For us (small catalogue, growing): browse by theme/energy
  (e.g. self-knowledge, voice, mind, spiritual), by author (Sayful Alvi first,
  then other authors), and by format (PDF / hardcopy / audio). The structure
  scales from 1 book to many without redesign.
- **"Best seller" and "In stock" as browse filters.** Simple, honest filters a
  buyer understands. For us: "বেস্ট সেলার" (once real sales exist), "স্টকে আছে",
  and format filters — never invented bestseller badges.
- **Pre-order as a first-class section.** A book can be listed before it is
  ready. For us: upcoming books/courses can be listed as pre-order/coming soon
  without pretending they are available.
- **Cash on delivery + simple ordering (name, phone, address).** The Bangladeshi
  book buyer's expected flow. Our order flow (cart → order confirmation, no
  payment UI for now) should mirror this simplicity: name, phone, address, and
  format choice — no account wall before ordering.
- **Category names in plain Bangla.** "আত্ম-উন্নয়ন, মোটিভেশনাল ও মেডিটেশন" is
  exactly the shelf our books sit on; plain Bangla category names beat clever

- **Non-book sprawl.** Electronics, gadgets, kitchen appliances, fashion, food,

- **Aggressive discount campaigns** ("up to 70% OFF", promo codes, gift

- **Cloudflare/bot-protection friction on the homepage.** The homepage text

  readable and light with no such barrier.

## 7. Islamic Online Madrasah (IOM) — iom.edu.bd

**What it is:** Online Islamic education platform (Bangla), "one of the largest

Relevant because our audience includes Muslim learners and our site has spiritual
lessons — and because IOM is the closest local example of a *learning journey*
presented as a staged path, not a course list.

  not name; video not shown to teachers; separate recitation groups for sisters
  led by female instructors; niqab not required for class.
- Course pages (seen via search): full syllabus by module, duration, fees,
  "admission open" status.

**Worth borrowing — and why:**
- **"For every stage of your journey" framing.** Courses are presented as a
  staged path (short → long, beginner → advanced), not a flat list. This is the
  closest local model for our 6-stage journey and the Four Energy progression.
  Present our path the same way: assessment → self-tests → one energy → next
  energy → Personal Project Plan → spiritual lessons.
- **Duration on every course card.** Every card states its length plainly
  (6 months, 3 years…). For us: duration/lessons on every course and book
  card, so a visitor can judge commitment at a glance.
- **Numbered "Why enrol here" reasons (01–08).** Concrete, checkable reasons
  (live classes, recordings, small-group practice, extra support for weak
  students, certificate). For us: a "Why Energy Mining Academy" strip with our
  own true reasons (free assessment, four energies, Bangla-first, real teachers,
  no score/ranking) — numbered, plain, verifiable.
- **Extra-support system for struggling students (AICU).** A named support
  mechanism for those who fall behind. For us: the equivalent is human support
  on the journey — check-ins, reminders, a help/contact route — not a
  "you failed" state. Worth designing as a support section, not a remedial label.
- **Scholar/teacher endorsements with names and faces.** Real named people
  vouching, not anonymous quotes. For us: teacher profiles with real bios are
  the trust anchor (Sayful Alvi + each course teacher) — no anonymous
  testimonials.
- **Inclusive-by-design details stated plainly.** Roll-number (not name) joining,
  separate groups where appropriate, no dress requirement for class. For us: the
  same plain-language inclusion — Muslim + non-Muslim, all ages, spiritual
  lessons optional — stated on the site, not assumed.
- **Notice board.** A simple dated announcements strip (admissions open, new
  course, schedule). Cheap to build, useful for a small academy; fits our
  "what's new" need without a blog.

**Avoid — and why:**
- **Large trust numbers as the hero** (177119+ students). Ours must be only
  numbers we can verify; a big-number hero we cannot support would be invented.
- **Religious-authority framing as the brand.** IOM's authority comes from board
  recognition and named scholars. Our authority comes from the method (assessment
  → energies → plan) and the teacher; we must not borrow religious endorsement
  language, and our spiritual lessons stay optional and inclusive, never
  sectarian or madhhab-specific.
- **Long-programme commitment language** (3-year / 7-year courses). Our courses
  are shorter self-development programmes; do not copy multi-year framing.
- **Admission-fee + monthly-fee display per course** as the card's main data.
  Our fees are not set; and the assessment must never show a fee at all.
