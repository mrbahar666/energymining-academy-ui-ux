A six-stage journey

From knowing yourself to changing yourself — one stage after another.

  1. Stage 1

    My formation

    Life Timeline — see your own story from the beginning.

  2. Stage 2

    Knowing and understanding myself

    Self-test, 360° Mirror, Human Types, Strengths and the Four Energy Map.

  3. Stage 3

    Vision and Present

    Make clear where you want to go, and where you are now.

  4. Stage 4

    Development

    Four Energy courses — working on Voice, Body, Mind and Purpose.

  5. Stage 5

    Cleansing

    The path of letting go of what you have been holding on to.

  6. Stage 6

    Spiritual path

    An optional path — for those who want it.
